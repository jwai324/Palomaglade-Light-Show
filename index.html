        Cheap custom extension cords

        SPT-1 wire can be used with vampire plugs bought on Amazon to create cheap custom extension cords, cut to exactly the length you need. SPT-1 is flat two-wire “zip cord.” Sharp teeth inside each vampire plug bite through the wire’s plastic coating and touch the copper inside, so there’s no stripping and no tools.

        How to make one

        1. Measure and cut. Measure from the box to where your lights start, add a little slack, and cut the wire straight across. Don’t strip it.
        2. Find the ribbed side. One half of the wire has fine ridges on it. That half is neutral, and it always lines up with the wide prong or slot.
        3. Add the plug (the end that goes into the box). Open the plug, push the wire all the way in with the ribbed half on the wide-prong side, and press it closed firmly. The teeth pierce the coating.
        4. Add the socket (the end your lights plug into). Do the same on the other end. For several lights on one cord, snap inline “pass-through” sockets onto the wire wherever you need them, without cutting it.
        5. Check it. Tug each plug, make sure no copper shows, then plug in and test.

        Buy and use them safely

        • On Amazon, search for “SPT-1 wire” and “SPT-1 vampire plugs” (also sold as slide-on or snap-on plugs). Get plugs labeled SPT-1: SPT-2 plugs are made for thicker wire and won’t grip SPT-1 properly.
        • Unplug the cord before adding or moving a plug.
        • Stay under the amp rating printed on the plug’s package. Don’t use these cords for heaters or other high-power devices.
        • Keep connections off the ground and out of puddles.
